# Issue #26 (frozen spec — source of truth for the overnight run; canonical: https://github.com/jptrsn/owntone_remote/issues/26)

Elapsed/duration must share the playhead row; slider flexes between them

## Defect
Elapsed time and total duration do not share the playhead row; the slider stretches full-width, pushing the times elsewhere (and they may wrap/displace on long text).

## Expected
One row: `[elapsed] ——————playhead—————— [total]` — times always on the playhead's row; the slider takes only the **flex space between** them and never squeezes the time labels.

## Where
`lib/src/ui/now_playing_screen.dart` (seek-bar layout).

## Repro / verification
Widget test with a long title + 3-digit-minute durations (10:00+) asserting the three render in one `Row` with the slider `Expanded`; screenshot compare on a disposable AVD at a narrow width.
